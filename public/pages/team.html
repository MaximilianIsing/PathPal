Our Team

Brody Rosenblatt

Co-Founder

I co-founded PathPal because I wanted to solve the same problems I faced as a high school student. I remember anxiously waiting to see if I would get into my dream school with almost no insight or real guidance. That experience showed me how stressful and confusing the college process can be when you're navigating it blindly. With PathPal, we set out to give students the clarity, structure, and confidence that I wish I had. Our goal is to make academic and career planning more transparent, more personalized, and ultimately more empowering for every student.

Andrew Schwarzman

Co-Founder

I've always believed big goals shouldn't feel overwhelming — you just need a clear path. As a student, I aimed high but had to navigate admissions, major changes, and internships on my own. That showed me how stressful the journey can be without real guidance. I helped build Pathpal to fix that — to give students the personalized blueprint I never had. A tool that shows where you are, where you want to go, and the steps to get there. My mission is simple: no one should have to guess their way toward their future.
